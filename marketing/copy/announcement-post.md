## LinkedIn (long form) — image: `marketing/images/social-linkedin.png` (1200 × 627)

Meet NextMind Desktop: a free, open-source desktop organizer for Windows.

The Windows desktop quickly turns into a pile of icons. NextMind Desktop gives it structure. You create named zones right on the desktop, drop files, folders and shortcuts into them, collapse the ones you do not need, and carry on.

What is new in v0.2.0:
• Search in every zone: type, and that zone's items filter live
• You decide the layout: drag zones anywhere, snap to neighbors (hold Alt to move freely), overlap them if you like
• Smarter expanding, and menus that stay out of the way

It is small, quiet and local-first: no account, no cloud, no telemetry. Licensed under Apache-2.0.

Windows 10 (2004+) and Windows 11, x64.
Download: [link]
Source: github.com/NextMindHQ/nextmind-desktop

If you try it, tell us what you would improve.
