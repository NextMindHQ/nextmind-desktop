# Messaging: NextMind Desktop

Draft marketing copy (English). Not published. Everything here describes what the app actually does; features marked **(v0.2)** ship with the next release.

## Name and tagline
- **Product:** NextMind Desktop (the name does not change)
- **Headline:** Your desktop. Your rules.
- **Signature line:** Free. Open source. Made for Windows.

## One-liners
- **Shortest:** Collapsible zones for your Windows desktop. Free and open source.
- **Short (about 25 words):** NextMind Desktop is a free, open-source desktop organizer for Windows: collapsible zones that group your files, folders and shortcuts right on the desktop.
- **Medium (about 60 words):** The Windows desktop quickly turns into a pile of icons. NextMind Desktop gives it structure: create named zones right on the desktop, drop files, folders and shortcuts into them, collapse the ones you do not need, and search inside any zone (v0.2). It is small, quiet and local-first: no account, no cloud, no telemetry.

## Feature bullets (all true today, v0.2 where marked)
- Named zones that live on the desktop itself: above the wallpaper and icons, below your applications
- You decide the layout: drag zones anywhere, overlap them if you like; edges snap to neighbors, hold Alt to move freely **(v0.2)**
- Collapse a zone to just its title; expanding opens downward or upward, whichever covers fewer other zones **(v0.2)**
- Search in every zone: live filter, case- and diacritics-insensitive, keyboard-friendly (↓ ↑ Enter Esc) **(v0.2)**
- Drag and drop files, folders and shortcuts; sort by name, date added or type; manual ordering
- Native Windows icons, tray icon and Control Center, Start with Windows
- Show desktop (Win+D) reveals the zones
- Local-first: no account, no cloud, no telemetry; Apache-2.0

## Boundaries: what we do not claim
- No AI, no cloud sync, no accounts, no analytics
- No global search yet (search works inside one zone at a time); it matches names, not file contents
- Zones never float above application windows; Show desktop reveals them
- Windows 10 (2004+) and Windows 11, x64 only; the executable is not code-signed yet (SmartScreen may warn)
- Do not call it "unlimited", "fastest" or "the best"; "without limits" is a slogan about the layout (no grid), not a technical claim

## Tone
Calm, confident, plain English. Short sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks, no neon.
