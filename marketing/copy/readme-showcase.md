```markdown
# NextMind Desktop

**Your desktop. Your rules.**
A free, open-source desktop organizer for Windows: collapsible zones on your desktop, below your windows and above your icons.

[⬇ Download the latest release](https://github.com/NextMindHQ/nextmind-desktop/releases/latest)

## Why NextMind Desktop

| | |
|---|---|
| <img src="marketing/images/zones.png" alt="Expanded and collapsed zones" width="420"> | **Organize without limits.** Create named zones, drop files, folders and shortcuts into them, collapse what you do not need. Zones stay exactly where you put them, also after a restart. |
| <img src="marketing/images/search.png" alt="Search in a zone" width="420"> | **Find it. Instantly.** Type in any zone and its items filter as you type. Works with Polish letters and mixed case; ↓ ↑ Enter Esc for the keyboard. |
| <img src="marketing/images/organization.png" alt="Several zones on a desktop" width="420"> | **Make space for what matters.** Drag & drop, sort by name, date or type, and a layout that is entirely yours: no grid, no auto-arranging. |

Local-first: **no account, no cloud, no telemetry.** Apache-2.0. Windows 10 (2004+) / Windows 11, x64.
```
